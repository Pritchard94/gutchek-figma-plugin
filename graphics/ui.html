How to write a brief

Start with campaign defaults, then one heading per format. Each heading takes Headline:, Body:, CTA:, Visual:, Theme: (brand, light or dark), Eyebrow: and Link:.

Formats: Instagram post, portrait, carousel, story · WhatsApp status · TikTok cover · X, Facebook and LinkedIn posts · Website share image · Email header · App Store screenshots · Google Play screenshots and feature graphic · Meta ads · Google display (all IAB sizes) · Full set.

Visuals: meter, chat, notes, rooms, gauge, logo, quote: …, phone: Home, phones: Home, Report overview. Phones use screens from the Screens · Light page.

Carousels and store screenshots: one numbered line per slide, for example 1. A fair read, with the receipts | phone: Report overview | theme: light.
